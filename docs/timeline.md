# Timeline and research on the case

## Questions

1. What role does the Angelini group play in Arauco's strategy, expansion and legitimacy?
2. Do Arauco's technological and industrial investments transform the production model or adapt it to survive?
3. How has economic concentration sustained the continuity of Chile's forestry model?

**Working hypothesis.** Sustainability, certification and innovation strategies work mainly as mechanisms of institutional legitimacy that sustain the extractive-export model under growing territorial, environmental and regulatory pressure.

## Base timeline (from the essay)

| Year | Theme | Event |
|---|---|---|
| 1939 | State and regulation | CORFO, the state development agency, is created to lead industrialisation. |
| 1967 | State and regulation | CORFO founds Celulosa Arauco, a pulp mill in the Biobío region. |
| 1969 | State and regulation | CORFO founds Celulosa Constitución. |
| 1974 | State and regulation | Decree Law 701 subsidises up to 75% of the cost of planting pine and eucalyptus; industrial plantations grow from under 300 thousand hectares in the 1970s to over 2.5 million by 2000. |
| 1979 | Group, ownership and investment | In the wave of privatisations, Empresas Copec (Angelini group) acquires Celulosa Arauco y Constitución; the brand Arauco is born. |
| 1990 | Group, ownership and investment | Return to democracy; the export model and the large business groups consolidate, and pulp becomes one of Chile's main industrial exports. |
| 1996 | Group, ownership and investment | International expansion begins: assets in Argentina, later Brazil, Uruguay and the United States. |
| 2004 | Territory and environment | The Valdivia mill starts up; the Río Cruces wetland crisis that follows becomes a landmark environmental conflict. |
| 2012 | State and regulation | The planting subsidies of Decree Law 701 expire. |
| 2017 | Territory and environment | The largest forest fires on record burn central-southern Chile. |
| 2022 | Sustainability and legitimacy | MAPA, the modernisation and expansion of the Arauco mill, starts operating: the case for technology as adaptation of the extractive model. |
| 2023 | Territory and environment | New large forest fires in Biobío and Ñuble. |
| 2024 | Sustainability and legitimacy | Sustainability-linked financing: a sustainable financing framework and sustainability bond issuance. |

New events after the essay are recorded in the [case log](log.md).
